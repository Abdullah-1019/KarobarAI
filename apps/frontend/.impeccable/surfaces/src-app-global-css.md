# Surface brief — Haveli design-system foundation (Batch 0)

Scope: app-wide visual foundation (tokens, type, AntD bridge, core components, shells). Modes: Persuade on the storefront home and /sell; Operate on cart/checkout/orders/seller/admin. Plan of record: docs/frontend/WORKLOG.md (Batch 0, steps 0.1–0.8). Redesign: the old green/cream/Jakarta world is evidence and anti-reference only.

Unresolved: real product photography (placeholders redrawn for now); dark mode polish deferred.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Lahore haveli at golden hour — espresso wood, sandstone ground, copper hardware, brass inlay — so a small unknown seller looks established and trustworthy. Refuses the category default: green-on-white marketplace chrome and Daraz flash-sale orange.

OWN-WORLD: Parchment/Cream/Sand grounds with Linen hairlines; Espresso ink carries headings and every dark band (announcement, hero, footer) with Walnut as its second tone; Copper is the single "do this" colour; Sindoor only for urgency; Brass as jewellery (AI mark, verified, New) under ~3%; Indigo ink the one cool note. Fraunces display over Inter; Urdu in Naskh for UI, Nastaliq for display. Block-print (ajrak/ralli) geometry at ~6% opacity is the recurring material.

STORY: Buyer sees a crafted, calm bazaar → trusts the seller → acts on one copper button. Seller/admin see a quiet, dense, warm workspace where brand lives in precise details.

FIRST VIEWPORT: (Batch 0 does not recompose pages.) Every existing first viewport must read espresso/parchment/copper: espresso announcement band, cream header, existing hero rendered espresso→walnut with brass hairline and block-print, one copper primary per screen.

FORM: Pinned by the user's brief (Haveli palette, Fraunces + Inter, plan in docs/frontend/WORKLOG.md). A pinned direction beats the roll, so concept-seed was deliberately not run. seed: none (user-pinned direction, no roll)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
