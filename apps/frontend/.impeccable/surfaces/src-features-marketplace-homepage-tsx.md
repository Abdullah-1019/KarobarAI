# Surface brief — Home (/) storefront

Scope: buyer Home, Persuade mode, inside the established Haveli world (DESIGN.md). Audience: Pakistani buyers, many first-time online shoppers, mostly on phones. Job: find something and trust a small seller enough to order. Proof on hand: real categories and products from the home feed; platform guarantees (COD, tracking, returns window, verified sellers). Constraints: no invented ratings, sellers, prices or stats; header search defers to the hero on Home.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A bazaar's front step, search-led — the box you type into is the hero's primary action, with real categories one tap away — refusing the category default of a promo carousel or a "Shop now" button over a stock banner.

OWN-WORLD: Haveli (DESIGN.md): espresso→walnut band with masked brass block-print and brass hairline, cream search with copper action, illustrated product cards on cream with espresso shadow-ink depth, tonal category tiles carrying the same illustrations.

STORY: Visitor sees what this is (Pakistani sellers, cash on delivery, tracking) → types or taps a category → browses featured and new products → sellers find the one invitation to /sell.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left (inline-start): Fraunces headline (max 15ch), one-line promise, 62px cream search with copper submit, "Try a category" chips (real categories), brass "Sell on KarobarAI" link. Right: a fanned stall of three illustrated product cards with a brass AI tag and a cream "Pay on delivery" tag. Trust strip straddles the band's lower edge. Primary action = the search.

FORM: Marketplace/directory pattern (search-led hero → categories → featured → new → seller CTA), taken from ui-ux-pro-max's landing data for marketplaces, inside the pinned world. seed:4b39a33361350d13 (rolled for the record; the user-pinned Haveli direction and approved page plan govern — the dealt structures were not adopted)
